Method

A counting clone of the vendored R counts vector allocations and vector copies, with bytes, in one benchmark call after the warmup, minus an empty call. FIŘ counts come from the timed modules. Totals use the 133 examples with a count in every build (no count: shootout_pidigits_orbit).

Counting method in detail
  • A clone of the vendored R (in the scratch folder, not in the repository) counts vector allocations (allocVector3) and vector copies (duplicate, shallow_duplicate), by type and length bucket, with bytes. Copied bytes are the bytes allocated during a copy, so a deep copy counts its elements. Only the outermost copy counts.
  • A count is one benchmark call after the example's warmup, minus an empty call. The second call makes the same copies in every example.
  • FIŘ counts come from the timed modules themselves. So the count of FIŘ's own copies (fir.dups of the statistics build) is not cross-checked.
  • GNU-R's duplicate_counter is not used: of its 969 K counts on bc.opt, 672 K are copies of argument lists (do_summary, do_logic3), not vector data.
  • Validation on micro programs (bytecode level 3): x <- x * 2 + 1 on a vector of length 100 makes 1 allocation per iteration; on a scalar 0; vec <- swap(vec, 1L, 2L) makes 1 copy per call; structure(a, k = k) on 64 or more elements makes an ALTREP wrapper, and the data copy happens at the first write.
  • Known gaps: ALTREP objects (1:n, wrappers) are not allocations. The R function of a copy site comes from the context chain, so a site in a frame that FIŘ elides shows its caller.
  • The copy classes pair copies per example by R function, type and length bucket, then by type and length bucket. So a removed and an added copy of the same shape in one example cancel, and the "only" classes are lower bounds.
  • Class (e) (neither makes the copy) was not counted. It needs another run that counts in-place writes.

The geometric mean ratios use the smoothing of analyze.py: copies +1, copied bytes +1024, allocations +1, allocated bytes +1, over the examples above. MB is 220 bytes.

Timing method
  • On this Mac (Apple silicon, 12 cores, 24 GB), OptimizationBenchmark, DEBUG=false, R_EVAL_TIMEOUT=600, modules reused (IGNORE_SNAPSHOTS='snapshot/bench/opt').
  • Round 1: CE0, CE1, CE2, TIP (with the variant without inlineInternalWrapper), GVN (with the variant without globalValueNumbering). Round 2: TIP, CE2, CE1, CE0.
  • Each run waits for a 1-minute load below 3 and no other Maven process (up to 15 min).
  • Method as in the 2026-11-16 report (../README.md): the bytecode time of an example is the median of its bc.opt time over all runs; the load factor of a run is the geomean over the examples and the three GNU-R rows of the time divided by the median over all runs; a FIŘ cell is min(<none>, <none> (rerun)) divided by the load factor. A build with two rounds uses the geomean of its two rounds. The noise floor of a variant comparison is the geomean of max(r, 1/r), r = rerun/none.
  • Earlier result (2026-11-25 morning, same three commits, two rounds, now not kept): CE1/CE0 1.008, CE2/CE1 0.994, CE2/CE0 1.001 geomean.

On this page every time ratio is a time divided by another time: below 1 is faster. The noise band of one example is the largest spread of its runs: max(r, 1/r) with r = rerun / none, and round 2 / round 1 of a build with two rounds. A ratio inside the band is neutral. The noise floor of a mean is the geometric mean of these bands.

Recommendation of the coordinator

The timings rank the candidates by the gap that they close, not only by the boxes or copies that they remove. Recommended order (proposals; nothing is implemented):

  1. First fix the feedback of the examples that stop at a connection. This is a fix of the benchmark harness, not an optimization. The feedback interpreter stops at the first call of rawConnection or textConnection (unregistered stubs). In the 8 such examples whose largest boxing blocker is "no type speculation", the source creates the connection directly before its hot loops (mandelbrot_orbit: line 24, loops from line 25). So these loops get no feedback, and their versions have no type speculation. mandelbrot_orbit is the slowest example (6.13× bc.opt), mandelbrot_naive is 1.54×; the others are the 4 knucleotide examples (1.04–1.16×), mandelbrot and reversecomplement_2. Until the fix, their timings measure code without speculation, and the rsh JIT, which records with rcp, has feedback there. Proposal: let the GNU-R fallback create a connection and keep it as an opaque value, then time these examples again. Size not estimated. In all, 43 of 134 examples stop early (26 at the cost budget of the GNU-R fallback); for a budget stop, the position against the hot loop was not checked.
  2. Then candidate 2, first half: in SpecializeNonReflectiveEnv, ignore a symbol promise whose symbol is not a parameter.
