## Copies that only FIŘ makes (from the copy classes)

- An unconditional `Fir_dup`: 110,903 copies, 34 MB. `Fir2CCompiler` emits it when a `dup` does not match its `SetterPattern`; GNU-R tests the count first and writes in place. Examples: `nbody_naive` `advance`, `body_vx[[i]] <<- body_vx[[i]] - …` (75,000 copies of 5 doubles); `revcomp` `exchange`, `substr(data[i], j, j) <<- …` (14,999 copies, 29 MB); `fannkuchredux`, `perm[k_range] <- rev(perm[k_range])` (15,285).
- The builtin `[<-` or `[[<-` copies a target that it sees as shared: `do_subassign_dflt` 31,003 (`nbody_naive_2` `body_v[, i] <<- …` 25,000), `do_subassign2_dflt` 2,506 copies, 48 MB (`spectralnorm_alt_4`, `m[[i, j]] <- eval_A(i, j)`, a 20 KB matrix 2,500 times). Probable cause: the target is counted while the builtin runs (read in the source, not checked by a run).

## Candidate improvements (proposals; not implemented)

1. Unboxed versions of builtins that lack them: declare `%%` and `%/%` in `builtins.fir` (`I,I`, `R,R`, `I,R`, `R,I`, and `v1` forms) with C in `runtime_inline.h` (GNU-R's rules for `NA`, a zero divisor, the sign), and in the interpreter (`Builtins.java`); add mixed `I,R`/`R,I` versions of `==` and `!=`; specialize `[<-` of an integer value into a real vector. Reach: about 4 M boxes (16 % of the excess), the largest boxing blocker in about 15 examples whose time at `TIP` is 81–172 % of `bc.opt` (`gcd` 172 %, `fasta_c` 121 %, `int_vec_add_t1` 119 %, `binarytrees_2` 115 %). Estimate: 5–20 % on these, about 1–2 % geomean (not measured). Risk low; size about 150–250 lines in 3–4 files plus tests.
2. Loop variables out of the environment: in `SpecializeNonReflectiveEnv`, ignore a symbol promise whose symbol is not a parameter of the function (about 10 lines; `missing()` in the callee follows a symbol promise to the caller's binding, so the rule must also require that no store to the variable can store the missing argument, as in `n <- quote(expr = )`); in `PromoteStaticallyKnownVariables`, promote a variable that is maybe-unbound at the loop header, with an "unbound" sentinel in the deopt write-back (medium to large, higher risk). Reach: 54 % of the excess boxes (`fib`, `mmm_t1`, `fannkuchredux_naive`, `convolution*`, `int_vec_add`). First run the pass with debug output on `fib` to confirm the reject condition.
3. A conditional `dup` (copy side): test the count before the copy, as GNU-R does, where a `dup` does not match `SetterPattern`; and check why `[<-`/`[[<-` see their target as shared. Reach: about 144 K of the 146 K copies that only FIŘ makes (`nbody*`, `revcomp`, `fannkuchredux`, `spectralnorm_alt_4`).
4. Promises (lower ceiling: removing `promiseInlining` cost only 1.02× in the 2026-11-16 report): step 2 of `proposal-unobserved-promises.md` (move a promise's leading speculation into the caller's checkpoint) for `spectralnorm*` and `crt`, about 38 % of the promises created.
